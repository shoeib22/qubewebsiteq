Acoustic Engineering

+

The room itself is your most critical audio component. We physically tune your space using advanced modeling to eliminate distortion, standing waves, and echo, ensuring pristine audio clarity.

BASS MANAGEMENT

Calculated low-frequency corner trapping to absorb standing waves and tighten bass response.

REFLECTION CONTROL

Broadband absorption panels placed precisely at first-reflection points to stop sound smearing.

SPATIAL DIFFUSION

Geometric rear-wall diffusers that scatter sound waves, creating a massive, natural soundstage.
